About & what this can't do

What it does: keeps your review link and business details on this device, writes the request text for you, opens your Messages app, logs every request with its date, flags 2-day reminders, and holds reply templates. Everything is stored only in this browser on this device (local storage) — nothing leaves the phone except the texts you send yourself.

Can't do (yet): send texts by itself (needs the Phase 2 platform — shown grey above); read your Google reviews automatically (same); print the QR while offline (the QR maker loads online, on demand only). If a text doesn't reach a customer, this tool can't see that — your Messages app is the record.
